Day-by-Day Pilgrimage Overview

◆
DAY 1

Mangalore to Udupi

MANGALORE • UDUPI

Afternoon arrival at Mangalore. Proceed to Udupi, check in at hotel & seek evening darshan at famous Udupi Sri Krishna Temple.

DAY 2

Udupi to Kollur Mookambika

ANEGUDDE • KOLLUR

Morning Anegudde Ganesha Temple darshan. Proceed to Kollur for a sacred 3-hour Homa ritual & evening grand Mookambika Darshan.

DAY 3

Kollur to Sringeri & Dharmasthala

SRINGERI • HORANADU

Scenic drive to Sringeri Sharadamba & Horanadu Annapoorneswari Temples, followed by evening Darshan at Dharmasthala.

DAY 4

Dharmasthala to Kukke & Kateel

KUKKE • KATEEL

Kukke Subramanya darshan & Kateel Sri Durgaparameswari river-island temple. Pilgrimage concludes with drop-off.

Detailed Yatra Itinerary & Timings
DAY 1: MANGALORE – UDUPI

• Afternoon: Arrival at Mangalore Airport or Railway Station

• Proceed to Udupi

• 01:00 PM: Lunch | 03:30 PM: Proceed to Udupi Sri Krishna Temple

• 04:00 PM – 07:00 PM: Darshan and Pooja

• 07:30 PM: Proceed to Hotel | 08:30 PM: Dinner

• Over Night Stay at Udupi

DAY 2: UDUPI – KOLLUR (MOOKAMBIKA)

• 06:00 AM: Wake up Call | 07:00 AM: Breakfast

• 07:30 AM: Proceed to Anegudde Sree Ganesha Temple, Kumbashi

• 08:30 AM – 09:30 AM: Darshan and Pooja

• 09:45 AM: Proceed to Kollur Mookambiga Temple

• 10:45 AM: Arrive at Kollur (Homa to Goddess Mookambigai at Priest Homa Center) & Check-in at Hotel

• 12:45 PM: Completion of Homa

• 01:00 PM – 04:00 PM: Lunch & Rest

• 04:30 PM – 07:30 PM: Darshan and Pooja

• 08:30 PM: Dinner | Over Night Stay at Kollur

DAY 3: KOLLUR – SRINGERI – HORANADU – DHARMASTHALA

• 05:00 AM: Wake up Call | 06:00 AM: Breakfast

• 06:30 AM: Proceed to Sringeri

• 09:15 AM: Arrive at Sringeri Sharadamba Temple

• 09:30 AM – 11:15 AM: Darshan and Pooja

• 11:30 AM: Proceed to Horanadu Annapoorneswari Temple

• 01:00 PM – 03:00 PM: Darshan and Pooja

• 03:00 PM: Proceed to Dharmasthala

• 06:30 PM: Check in at Hotel in Dharmasthala

• 07:00 PM: Proceed to Manjunatha Swamy Temple

• 07:15 PM – 08:30 PM: Darshan and Pooja

• 08:45 PM: Back to Hotel | 09:00 PM: Dinner

• Over Night Stay at Dharmasthala

DAY 4: DHARMASTHALA – KUKKE SUBRAMANYA – KATEEL – MANGALORE

• 06:00 AM: Wake up Call | 07:00 AM: Breakfast

• 11:15 AM: Proceed to Mangalore for Lunch

• 03:00 PM: Proceed to Sri Durgaparameswari Temple, Kateel

• 04:15 PM: Arrive at temple

• 04:30 PM – 06:00 PM: Darshan and Pooja

• Trip Concludes: Drop at Airport or Railway Station or Hotel
